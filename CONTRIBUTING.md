# Contributing to Note Synapse

First off, thank you for your interest in Note Synapse! We are thrilled that you want to contribute.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

To ensure that we can legally distribute your contributions as part of Note Synapse, we require all contributors to sign a Contributor License Agreement (CLA).

*   **Grant of Rights**: By signing the CLA, you grant **Bruce Li** the right to distribute your work.
*   **Copyright Retention**: You **retain your copyright** to your contributions, and you are free to use your contributions elsewhere.

> [!NOTE]
> The CLA integration is currently **work in progress**. As a result, we will be able to accept contributions soon, once the system is fully operational.

## Forking and Hacking

In the meantime, you are more than welcome to **fork the repository** and hack around! We appreciate your enthusiasm and patience as we set up our contribution infrastructure.
