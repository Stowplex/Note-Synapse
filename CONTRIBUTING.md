# Contributing to Note Synapse

First off, thank you for your interest in Note Synapse! We are thrilled that you want to contribute.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

To ensure that we can legally distribute your contributions as part of Note Synapse, we require all contributors to sign a Contributor License Agreement (CLA). See [CLA.md](./CLA.md) for detailed information.

## Forking and Hacking

You are more than welcome to **fork the repository** and hack around as long as your work abide by the terms of the [LICENSE](./LICENSE).
